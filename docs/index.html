Read the implementation decisions.

Three field notes with executable fictional examples, observed failures and the work still required for a real installation.

  • Build a support assistant that can decline to answer

    See why approved answer text can still be irrelevant, and test where a person must take over.

  • Keep AI editorial review tied to its sources

    Compare a fabricated quote with a genuine quote attached to a misleading claim.

  • Make public-source research stop and resume predictably

    Inspect discovery replay, duplicate content and a budget pause that survives a restart.

These are reusable starting points with fictional examples. Synthetic checks are separate from live provider acceptance, production delivery and business results. Read the validation boundaries before connecting your accounts. GitHub publication does not mean every template is approved by n8n.
